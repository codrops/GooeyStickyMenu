Aloe grows slowly in the black volcanic soil of northern Lanzarote, with little rain and a steady wind. We cut a few outer leaves from each plant by hand, early in the morning, and open them the same day. The gel inside is clear and cool, and it goes into the jar much as it comes out of the leaf.
